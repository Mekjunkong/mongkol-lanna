# MONGKOL LANNA Design Context

## Physical scene
A customer holds a phone in warm evening light and quietly explores a small digital gallery, seeing a personal artwork large enough to desire before reading the story behind it.

## Direction
Contemporary art gallery × Northern Thai atelier × quiet luxury. Artwork dominates and interface disappears around it.

## Palette
Warm ivory, aged paper white, deep jade, charcoal, muted vermillion, and very restrained antique gold. Avoid pure black/white. Gold is a directional accent, not mystical decoration.

## Typography
Editorial, calm, premium, large titles, generous leading, short readable paragraphs. Avoid software-dashboard language on customer surfaces.

## Layout
Mobile-first at 375px. Primary CTA visible. One-thumb creation flow with touch targets at least 44px. Avoid card grids when editorial spreads, artwork crops, lists, and dividers communicate better.

## Ornament
Use Lanna ornament only at section transitions, frames, small dividers, Art Passport, and selected premium details. Never decorate every card.

## Motion
Slow artwork reveal, gentle opacity transitions, subtle line movement, optional very gentle parallax. Respect reduced motion. No particles, sparkles, dramatic 3D, or excessive scroll effects.

## Avoid
Large black surfaces, glowing gold, gradients, glassmorphism, purple AI color, robot/magic/sparkle icons, SaaS metrics, nested cards, generic dashboards, fake sacred motifs.

## Phone previews
Realistic lock and home screen states with simulated time/date, notch/Dynamic Island safe area, icon area, and controls ARTWORK ⇄ LOCK SCREEN / HOME SCREEN. Important artwork content stays outside conflicts.

## Artwork Explorer
After full artwork reveal, offer Explore the Story. Interactive details should feel like a small digital museum. Use neutral visual observations such as “Gold is concentrated near the destination, creating increasing visual warmth.” Never invent religious claims.
