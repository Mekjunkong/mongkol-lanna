# MONGKOL LANNA Product Context

## Register
brand

## Product purpose
MONGKOL LANNA is a personal Lanna-inspired story-art atelier. It transforms a customer's life chapter, intention, and optional story into art direction and a personalized digital artwork. The customer should feel they are commissioning art, never configuring software or writing an AI prompt.

## Audience
Mobile-first customers arriving from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LINE, QR codes, and mobile ads. Initial use is digital lock-screen and home-screen art, with future gifting and fine-art print products.

## Core promise
Your story, composed as Lanna-inspired art. Meaning, story, art direction, Northern Thai identity, and personalization delivered as a beautiful artwork.

## Conversion goal
Within 10 seconds visitors understand what it is, what they do, what they receive, a visible starting price/test range, expected workflow, and where to see examples. Primary action: Create My Art Direction.

## Core journey
Desire → Story → Art Direction → Artwork → Meaning → Passport.

## P0
Reposition homepage; simplify create to four decisions; deterministic Story Interpreter UX; free Art Direction Blueprint; clear editable packages; story-based gallery; realistic lock/home screen preview; Mongkol Art Passport; improved reveal; remove generic SaaS/AI language.

## Cultural policy
Use three explicit labels: Cultural Reference, Artistic Interpretation, Personal Symbolism. Inspired by Northern Thai visual heritage, never claim every image is historically authentic Lanna art. Never invent religious claims, fake Pali, fake Lanna script, yantras, sacred text, historical claims, or automatically insert Buddha imagery. Do not impersonate or copy living artists.

## Product safety
REAL_GENERATION=false throughout UI/product development. Mock checkout only. Do not call paid providers or burn credits. Prices are editable test ranges, never commercial commitments. No public launch without Mike approval.

## Packages
Essential: Personal Art Direction, personalized artwork, lock screen, home screen; test range ฿390–590.
Signature: Essential plus Artwork Story, Art Passport, one Art Direction Adjustment, high-resolution digital artwork; test range ฿790–1,190.
Collector: Signature plus print-ready master, multiple aspect ratios, collector presentation, priority quality review; test range ฿1,490–2,490.

## Non-goals
No social network, chat, NFT/blockchain, marketplace, subscriptions, AR, advanced astrology, gamification, dozens of symbols, unlimited regenerations, or real generation/payment in P0.

## Success question
Does this make the customer feel like they are commissioning art? If it feels like software configuration, Canva, Midjourney, or cheap wallpaper shopping, change it.
